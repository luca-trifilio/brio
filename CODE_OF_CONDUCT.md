# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant v2.1**.

The full text is published at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Summary

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected behaviour**: be kind, constructive, and welcoming.  
**Unacceptable behaviour**: harassment, trolling, personal attacks, or publishing
others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/luca-trifilio/brio/security/advisories/new)
(it keeps the report confidential) or by contacting the maintainers directly.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
