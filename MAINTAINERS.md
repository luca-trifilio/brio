## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Review and merge pull requests in a timely manner
- Triage issues (apply labels, ask for clarification, close duplicates)
- Review and merge Release PRs opened by release-please
- Respond to security reports within 7 days (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))
- Uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no formal process yet. If you are a consistent contributor and
interested in taking on more responsibility, open a Discussion and let's talk.

## Decision making

For now, decisions are made by the lead maintainer. As the project grows,
this section will be updated to reflect a more collaborative process.
